# Sieve of Eratosthenes — Practice Problems Progression

## Curated Practice Sets

- **BEST PRACTICE SET**: [CSES Mathematics Section](https://cses.fi/problemset/)

---

## 1. Beginner Tier (Foundations & Factorization Lookups)

1. **[Counting Divisors](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1713)**
   - **Platform**: CSES
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: CSES Mathematics / Codeforces 1200
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Precomputing prime factorization via SPF, formula $d(N) = \prod (a_i + 1)$.
   - **Recommended Order**: 1

2. **[Common Divisors](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1081)**
   - **Platform**: CSES
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1300
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Reverse sieve: frequency array iterated from $\max(A)$ downwards.
   - **Recommended Order**: 2

3. **[Almost Prime](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/26/A)**
   - **Platform**: Codeforces
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 900
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Counting numbers with exactly 2 distinct prime factors.
   - **Recommended Order**: 3

---

## 2. Intermediate Tier (Multiplicative Functions & GCD Chains)

1. **[Prime Multiples](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/2185)**
   - **Platform**: CSES
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1500
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Sieve primes combined with Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion (PIE).
   - **Recommended Order**: 4

2. **[T-primes](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/230/B)**
   - **Platform**: Codeforces
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1300
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Numbers with exactly 3 divisors are squares of primes ($p^2$).
   - **Recommended Order**: 5

3. **[Noldbach problem](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/17/A)**
   - **Platform**: Codeforces
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1000 - 1100
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Sieve generation of primes and checking sums of adjacent primes.
   - **Recommended Order**: 6

---

## 3. Advanced Tier (Segmented Sieve & Möbius Inversion)

1. **[Divisor Analysis](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/2182)**
   - **Platform**: CSES
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1700
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Computing count, sum, and product of divisors modulo $10^9 + 7$ using Fermat's Little Theorem.
   - **Recommended Order**: 7

2. **[Counting Coprime Pairs](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/2417)**
   - **Platform**: CSES
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1900
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Möbius function $\mu(n)$ precomputed inside linear sieve to count coprime pairs via PIE.
   - **Recommended Order**: 8

3. **[Prime Gift](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/809/B)**
   - **Platform**: Codeforces
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1800
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Interactive search combined with prime structure.
   - **Recommended Order**: 9
