# Binary Search on Answer — Practice Problems Progression

## Curated Practice Progression

- **BEST PRACTICE SET**: [Codeforces EDU Binary Search Course Steps 2 & 3](https://codeforces.com/edu/course/2)

---

## 1. Beginner Tier (Foundations & Standard Direct Predicates)

1. **[Factory Machines](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1620)**
   - **Platform**: CSES
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: CSES Sorting and Searching / Codeforces 1200
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Monotonic time function, products produced in $T$ seconds, 64-bit bounds.
   - **Recommended Order**: 1
   - **Editorial**: Compute total products $\sum \lfloor T / k_i \rfloor \ge t$.

2. **[Array Division](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1085)**
   - **Platform**: CSES
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: CSES Sorting and Searching / Codeforces 1300
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Partitioning array into $K$ subarrays to minimize maximum sum.
   - **Recommended Order**: 2

3. **[Magic Powder - 1](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/670/D1)**
   - **Platform**: Codeforces
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1100
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Feasibility of baking $X$ cookies given ingredient constraints and magic powder.
   - **Recommended Order**: 3

---

## 2. Intermediate Tier (Greedy Checkers & Geometry)

1. **[Tasks and Deadlines](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1630)**
   - **Platform**: CSES
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1400
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Greedy predicate sorting combined with binary search optimization.
   - **Recommended Order**: 4

2. **[Hamburgers](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/371/C)**
   - **Platform**: Codeforces
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1400
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Cost function monotonicity, high bound selection ($R = 10^{13}$).
   - **Recommended Order**: 5

3. **[Poisoned Dagger](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1613/C)**
   - **Platform**: Codeforces
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1200 - 1300
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Damage accumulation per attack interval with overlap capping $\min(k, a_{i+1} - a_i)$.
   - **Recommended Order**: 6

---

## 3. Advanced Tier (Multi-Dimensional & Graph Predicates)

1. **[Flight Routes Check](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1682)**
   - **Platform**: CSES
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1600
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Binary search on graph capacity / edge weights with BFS/DFS reachability.
   - **Recommended Order**: 7

2. **[Multiplication Table](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/448/D)**
   - **Platform**: Codeforces
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1600
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Counting numbers $\le X$ in an $N \times M$ multiplication table in $O(N)$ time.
   - **Recommended Order**: 8

3. **[Maximum Median](https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/1201/C)**
   - **Platform**: Codeforces
   - **Difficulty / Rating**: 1400 - 1500
   - **Key Concepts Tested**: Monotonic operation budgeting to lift the median of an array.
   - **Recommended Order**: 9
